Learning Through Play

Meaningful Learning Experiences for Every Age and Stage

Children at Just Like Home grow through play, hands-on activities, creative exploration, positive relationships, and developmentally appropriate lessons. Our programs support the whole child, helping children build confidence, communication skills, independence, and a love of learning.

Creative Curriculum

Our classrooms use Creative Curriculum to support learning through meaningful activities, guided discovery, and age-appropriate lessons connected to real developmental goals.

Play-Based Learning

Children learn best when they are actively exploring. Through purposeful play, they build problem-solving skills, creativity, social confidence, and curiosity.

Kindergarten Readiness

Preschool children are supported with early literacy, math, social skills, emotional development, classroom routines, and independence to help prepare them for kindergarten.

Spanish and Baby Signing

Children are introduced to Spanish language exposure and baby signing, helping build early communication skills while celebrating diverse Lakewood family backgrounds.

Music, Art, and Movement

Songs, movement, crafts, creative projects, and gross motor activities help children express themselves, build coordination, and develop imagination.

Social-Emotional Support

Teachers use positive guidance and Love and Logic strategies to help children build emotional awareness, independence, problem-solving skills, and healthy relationships.
