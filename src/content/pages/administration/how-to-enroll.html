Enrollment Steps

Our Enrollment Process

Enrolling at Just Like Home Daycare Center is designed to be simple, clear, and stress-free. Whether you are enrolling an infant, preschooler, or school-age child, and whether you pay privately or through subsidy, our team guides you from start to finish.

Step 1: Schedule a Tour

Visit one of our four Lakewood campuses to meet our team, see classrooms, and ask questions. Schedule your tour here.

Step 2: Make Your Enrollment Decision

After your tour, let us know when you are ready. Our team is available for follow-up questions by phone or email.

Step 3: Complete the Payment Process

  • DSHS / WCCC: Call DSHS with our provider number to start authorization.
  • Military Childcare Aware: Complete CCA forms and obtain your cost verification form.
  • Tribal Subsidy: Contact your tribal program for authorization.
  • Private Pay: Submit deposit after approval to hold your child's spot.

Step 4: Complete Your Enrollment Packet

We provide all required forms for health history, emergency contacts, authorized pickups, program agreements, and DCYF-required documentation.

Step 5: Submit Your Completed Packet

Return your completed packet and supporting documents to your chosen campus. We review and confirm all details.

Step 6: Arrange Your Start Date

After packet approval, we finalize your start date, classroom placement, and teacher introduction for a smooth first day.
