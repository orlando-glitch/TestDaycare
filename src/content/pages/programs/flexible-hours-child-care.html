Program Overview

Flexible Daycare Hours in Lakewood, Open 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM

Finding dependable child care that fits your family's schedule can be challenging. Just Like Home Daycare Center was founded specifically to support working families. Since 2006, we have operated Monday through Friday from 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM, giving families throughout Pierce County a reliable, licensed child care option that fits around the real demands of modern work schedules.

Who We Serve

Built for Families Who Need More Than a 9-to-5 Daycare

Our flexible hours program isn't a separate offering, it's woven into the fabric of everything we do. All four of our Lakewood campuses operate on the same extended schedule, so regardless of which location your family chooses, you'll have access to quality child care from early morning through late evening. Our extended schedule serves a wide range of working families throughout Lakewood, Tacoma, and the broader Pierce County area.

Healthcare & Shift Workers

Nurses, EMTs, CNAs, hospital staff, and other healthcare professionals depend on reliable daytime child care to support their demanding schedules. We're open Monday through Friday from 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM to provide consistent, quality care.

Military Families Near JBLM

Active-duty service members stationed at Joint Base Lewis-McChord often face unpredictable schedules and non-standard hours. We accept Military Childcare Aware and understand the unique demands of military family life.

Hospitality, Retail & Service Industry

Restaurant workers, hotel staff, retail employees, and service industry professionals frequently have shifting schedules. We're open Monday through Friday from 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM to provide trusted, licensed child care during the day.

Transportation & Overnight Professionals

Truck drivers, transit workers, and delivery professionals often have demanding schedules. Our 7:30 AM opening means your child is cared for early, and our 6:00 PM closing provides reliable coverage through the standard workday.

Parents Working Multiple Jobs

When work doesn't stop at one job, child care shouldn't either. Our flexible hours and subsidy acceptance mean reliable care is accessible even for families managing tight budgets and demanding schedules.

What You Can Expect

Quality Care at Every Hour. Not Just During Business Hours

Full-day hours only mean something if the quality of care stays consistent from 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM. At Just Like Home, we maintain the same trained staff, structured routines, nutritious meals, and nurturing environment throughout the entire operating day. Children in our care receive age-appropriate learning activities, proper rest time, meals and snacks, and continuous adult supervision, regardless of whether it's 8 AM or 5 PM. Our staff are fully trained, background-checked, and committed to making every child feel safe and valued at every hour we're open.
