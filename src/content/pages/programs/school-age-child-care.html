Program Details

School-Age Care That's More Than Just Supervision

We believe after-school time should be productive, enriching, and, just as important, fun. Our school-age program is built around the idea that older children still need a safe, supportive environment where they can decompress, get their homework done, and engage with caring adults and peers. Our staff know each child by name and take a genuine interest in their growth, challenges, and interests.

Homework Help & Academic Support

Each afternoon, children have dedicated time to work on homework and school assignments with staff support available. We create a quiet, focused environment so kids can get their work done before heading home for the evening.

Enrichment Activities & Creative Play

After homework, children enjoy a variety of enrichment options, from art projects and STEM activities to music, movement, and team games. Our program keeps older kids engaged, active, and social every day after school.

Early Morning Care from 7:30 AM

Need to drop off when you head to work? We open at 7:30 AM every weekday, giving parents a trusted, safe place for their school-age children throughout the full school and workday.

After-School Care Until 6:00 PM

Our 6:00 PM closing time means parents have reliable after-school coverage for their school-age children, bridging the gap between school dismissal and the end of the standard workday.

Summer & Non-School Day Coverage

School breaks don't have to mean scrambling for coverage. Our school-age program includes care during summer, holidays, early-release days, and teacher work days, keeping your child's routine consistent year-round.

What to Expect

A Typical After-School Day at Just Like Home

After school drop-off, children check in with their teacher, have a healthy snack, and settle into homework time. Once schoolwork is complete, the afternoon opens up into enrichment activities, outdoor play, creative projects, and free time with friends. Evenings are calm and structured, giving kids a comfortable space to wind down before pickup. Parents receive updates and can communicate with staff anytime through the Kid Reports app.
