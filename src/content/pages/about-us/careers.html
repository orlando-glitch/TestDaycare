Work Somewhere That Feels Just Like Home

At Just Like Home Daycare Center, every child deserves a safe, nurturing, and inclusive environment where they feel valued, respected, and loved. Since 2006, we have built a team of educators and caregivers who are passionate about helping children grow through play-based learning and meaningful relationships. Across our four Lakewood campuses, we partner with families through open communication, mutual respect, and genuine care. If you believe early childhood education is one of the most important jobs in the world, we would love to hear from you.

Why Educators Choose Just Like Home

We are committed to building a workplace where educators feel supported, valued, and inspired to do their best work. Our philosophy is guided by one belief: when educators thrive, children thrive. We celebrate each child's unique background, culture, abilities, and experiences, and we create classrooms where kindness, safety, and curiosity are part of every day.

Our Motto: "Nurturing children with love, learning, and care, just like home."

What We're Looking For: Warm, dependable professionals who communicate respectfully with children, families, and teammates; maintain safe and organized classrooms; and bring a growth mindset to early childhood education.

Professional Growth and Support: We support continuous professional growth by encouraging lifelong learning, reflective practice, and adherence to best practices in early childhood education.

WE'RE HIRING

  • The Assistant Director oversees daily operations, supervises staff and children, ensures high-quality care standards, and manages the center in the Director's absence. This leadership role requires independent judgment, strong communication with families and staff, and a commitment to safe, inclusive, relationship-based care. Ideal candidates have ECE leadership experience and familiarity with DCYF regulations.

    Assistant Director - Requirements

    • Ability to pass a Washington State and FBI background check
    • First Aid/CPR/Bloodborne Pathogens certification
    • Food Handler's Card
    • TB Test (required prior to employment)
    • Initial 30 Hours STARS training
    • All other training required by DCYF
